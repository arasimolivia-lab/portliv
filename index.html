I'm a designer who likes problems that don't have an obvious shape yet — new products, awkward rebrands, the second draft nobody wanted to write. Most of my work lives somewhere between a brand system and a piece of software.

Before this I studied Rochester Institute of Technology in New Media Design, with a minor in Ad and PR, and worked with teams at HabitsGreek365. I care about type, restraint, and things that still feel good on the hundredth use.
